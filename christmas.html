The Conkerbury Detectives: A Conkerbury Christmas

by Inspector Cuthbert Conkerworth · Book 2

24 Advent mysteries and a Christmas Day finale for detectives aged 8 to 12.

It's December in Conkerbury, the snow is falling, and things keep going missing! Join Priya, Ollie, Ffion, Alfie and Biscuit the sausage dog for one gentle mystery every day of Advent. Then, on Christmas morning, the old silver star vanishes from the top of the church tree, and only the Secret Paper Chain can tell you who took it.

ABOUT BOOK 2

What's inside

25 Christmas cases

  • 24 Advent cases, one for every day from 1st December
  • A Christmas Day finale: The Case of the Missing Star
  • Harder as Christmas gets closer, from Rookie to Chief Inspector

Puzzles of every kind

  • Suspect cards, village maps and timelines
  • Logic grids, fibbing witnesses, a Christmas-lights code and a carol code
  • The Secret Paper Chain: one letter from nine cases unlocks the finale

Help when you need it

  • Hints for every case
  • Answers printed upside down
  • A detective certificate
  • A secret Boxing Day bonus case on this site

Do one case a day like an advent calendar, or race through the lot. You don't need to have read book 1 first.

OUR THREE PROMISES

Exactly one answer

Every case has one answer, and it's fair. A computer solver checked every single one.

Nothing scary

Just missing mittens, swapped puddings and a magpie with a taste for tinsel. Nobody gets hurt, ever.

Hints, not spoilers

Stuck? Get a nudge from the hints in the book, or from the answer checker on this site.
