ABOUT THE BOOK

About the book

The Conkerbury Detectives: The Case of the Missing Conker Champion by Inspector Cuthbert Conkerworth is a detective puzzle book for children aged 8 to 12. It has 30 short, illustrated mystery cases set in the made-up English village of Conkerbury, across one school year: conker season, the bake sale, bonfire night, the panto, Pancake Day, the village show and the summer fete.

What's inside

  • 30 cases that get harder as you go, from Rookie to Chief Inspector
  • Suspect cards, maps, timelines, logic grids, codes and fibbing witnesses
  • A Secret Ledger that unlocks the final mystery of the bell tower
  • Hints for every case, and answers printed upside down
  • A detective certificate to fill in

Who it's for

Puzzle-loving readers aged 8 to 12, for long car journeys, rainy half-terms and classroom brain-teasers. Nothing scary: just missing conkers, swapped cakes and vanished trophies.

Formats

Paperback (8.5 x 11 in, 156 pages, room to write in). Available now on Amazon.

For parents and teachers

Every case is a fair-play logic puzzle with exactly one answer, checked by a computer solver. Children practise careful reading, reasoning and elimination. Hints give a nudge without spoiling the answer. This website is free, with no adverts, cookies or sign-ups.
