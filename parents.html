For parents, carers & teachers

The Conkerbury Detectives Club is a puzzle book for children aged 8 to 12. This page is for grown-ups.

THE BOOK

Thirty short detective cases set in a British village: conker contests, bake sales, the fete, bonfire night and the panto. Children read the clues, cross out suspects and work out the one answer that fits.

Every case is checked by a computer program to make sure it has exactly one correct answer, and every clue is fair. The crimes are all gentle: missing conkers, swapped cakes, vanished trophies. No violence and nothing frightening.

The answers and hints are in the back of the book. This website is an optional extra.

SAFE BY DESIGN
  • No accounts, no log-ins and no names are needed to use the site.
  • Answers are checked on your child's own device. Nothing they type is sent or stored.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no adverts and no links out to social media.
  • The fonts are hosted on this site, so no third party sees visits.
