Privacy notice

FOR GROWN-UPS

The short version: this website doesn't collect any personal information.

This website

This notice covers conkerburydetectives.co.uk, the website for the Conkerbury Detectives puzzle books by Mark Hutchinson.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, forms that send data, cookies, analytics, adverts, tracking pixels or embedded social media. Answer codes and certificate names are handled entirely inside the browser and are never sent or stored.

Children

The site is designed with the UK Age Appropriate Design Code (the “Children's Code”) in mind: high privacy by default, no profiling, no nudges to share information and no data collected from children.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like every website host, GitHub may keep short-term technical logs (such as IP addresses) to keep the service secure. We don't use these to identify anyone.

Newsletter (not yet active)

A newsletter for adults may be added later. If it is, this notice will be updated first to explain what's collected, why, the email service used, and how to unsubscribe.

Contact

Questions about this notice or the site? Email hello@conkerburydetectives.co.uk.
